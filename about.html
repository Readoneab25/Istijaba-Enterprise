About Istijaba

Built by people who've stood at the edge of a pond at 6am.

Istijaba Enterprises started with a simple frustration: too many farmers were getting inconsistent feed and unreliable supply. We set out to fix that, one honest delivery at a time.

Our story

Istijaba Enterprises grew out of a small feed retail counter into a full supply operation for fish farmers and retailers. We saw the same problem repeat itself — farmers losing feed conversion efficiency to inconsistent pellet quality, or losing whole cycles to unhealthy stock bought from unverified sources.

So we built our own standard: test every batch, verify every source, and treat delivery timelines as a promise, not an estimate. That approach is still how we operate today, whether you're ordering five bags or fifty.
